Iceland on a budget: five hacks that actually work

Fuel, food, hot springs, and how to plan stops to cut costs without missing the magic.

Nordic Road trip 12/08/2025 6 min read

Hack 1: Plan your fuel stops strategically

Fuel in Iceland is expensive, but stations are sparse outside the Ring Road. Fill up in larger towns rather than at remote pumps. Share a rental car with other travelers to split fuel costs. Better yet, take the long-distance buses—much cheaper than driving.

  • Research ahead: Find fuel stations on Atlantik gas app before you drive.
  • Timing: Fill up early morning or during off-peak hours.
  • Alternative: Blablacar for shared rides, or buses for major routes.

Hack 2: Geothermal pools and hot springs over fancy spas

Skip the expensive Blue Lagoon. Instead, visit free natural hot springs like Reykjadalur (Steam Valley) or Sky Lagoon for a cheaper experience. Many remote geothermal areas are free and far more authentic than commercialized spa resorts.

Money-saving tips for Iceland

Buy groceries from Bonus supermarket instead of restaurants. Camp or stay in hostels (many have kitchen access). Pack snacks and make picnic lunches to enjoy while watching waterfalls and glaciers. Visit during shoulder season (May-June or August-September) for lower prices and fewer crowds.
