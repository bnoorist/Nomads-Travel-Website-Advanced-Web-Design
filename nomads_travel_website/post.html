48 hours in Lisbon: viewpoints, pastel de nata, and trams

A compact itinerary built for sunsets, steep streets, and small-budget wins.

Europe City break 08/22/2025 6 min read

Day 1: Old town energy, sunset viewpoints

Start in Baixa early while the streets are calm. Grab coffee and a light breakfast, then walk uphill toward Alfama. The goal is simple: hit one viewpoint, eat something memorable, and let the city set the pace.

  • Morning: Praça do Comércio, Rua Augusta, quick pastry stop.
  • Midday: Alfama lanes, small shops, the cathedral area.
  • Sunset: Miradouro da Graça or Miradouro de Santa Luzia.

Day 2: Belém classics and a relaxed finish

Take the tram or train out to Belém. You can do the iconic stops fast, then spend the rest of the day on the riverfront. Lisbon is best when you leave space to wander.

Budget tips that do not ruin the fun

Use a day travel card for public transport, eat where locals eat at lunch, and save your paid attraction for one place you really care about. The views are free.
