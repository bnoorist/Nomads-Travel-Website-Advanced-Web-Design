Marrakech essentials: souks, rooftop dinners, and riad life

What to eat, where to stay, and how to navigate the maze without stress.

Africa Food 09/30/2025 6 min read

Day 1: The medina maze and souks at sunset

Get lost in the medina early, navigate the souk corridors for leather goods and spices. Grab lunch at a hidden riad courtyard café. By afternoon, head to Jemaa el-Fnaa square to watch snake charmers and street performers as the sun sets over the palms.

  • Morning: Ben Youssef Mosque, leather souks, spice markets.
  • Lunch: Hidden riad restaurant with traditional tagine.
  • Evening: Jemaa el-Fnaa square for food stalls and atmosphere.

Day 2: Riads, rooftop dinners, and Saadian Tombs

Explore the quiet corners of the medina with its stunning riad architecture. Visit the Saadian Tombs for history. End the day with a rooftop dinner at a traditional riad, overlooking the Koutoubia Mosque lit up against the night sky.

Budget tips for Marrakech exploration

Negotiate prices in souks—it's expected and part of the experience. Stay in a budget riad for authentic atmosphere without the premium price. Eat at local warung and food stalls around Jemaa el-Fnaa instead of tourist restaurants. Walking is free and reveals the medina's hidden corners.
