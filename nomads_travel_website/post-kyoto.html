Kyoto in one week: temples, tea houses, and quiet mornings

A slow-travel guide to the city beyond the crowds with simple daily routes.

Asia Culture 11/20/2025 6 min read

Day 1: Fushimi Inari and bamboo forests at sunrise

Wake before dawn for the Fushimi Inari pilgrimage. Thousands of vermillion torii gates wind up the mountainside, and you'll have the trails nearly to yourself. Descend for late breakfast, then head to Arashiyama for the famous bamboo grove walk.

  • Early morning: Fushimi Inari shrine hike with sunrise.
  • Breakfast: Local udon or soba near the shrine.
  • Afternoon: Arashiyama bamboo forest, Tenryu-ji temple.

Day 2+: Temples, tea houses, and gion walks

Explore the golden pavilion (Kinkaku-ji) and other temples at your own pace. Spend afternoons in Gion geisha district watching the light change. Visit a traditional tea house for matcha and sweets. Kyoto rewards slow exploration and quiet moments.

Budget tips for exploring Kyoto

Get a Kyoto Bus Pass for unlimited transport. Many temples are free or under ¥600 each. Eat at local ramen shops and convenience stores for affordable meals. Stay in a hostel or budget ryokan slightly outside the center and use transit to explore. Walking between neighborhoods is free and often more rewarding than paid attractions.
