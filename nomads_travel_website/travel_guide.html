Travel Guides

Simple tips before you go

These are just basic guides we made to help plan trips. Not everything applies to every place, but it’s a good start.

Quick topics

Last updated: kinda recently
Packing

Bring layers, even if you think you won’t need them. Also pack a charger (sounds obvious, but yeah).

Budget

Set a daily budget and try not to spend everything on day 1. Local food is usually cheaper and better.

Safety

Keep copies of your passport, watch your bag in crowded areas, and don’t trust “free” stuff too quickly.

Mini checklist

  • Passport + ID (and a photo copy)
  • Offline maps downloaded
  • Emergency contact written somewhere
  • Bank card works internationally (hopefully)

This list isn’t complete, but it covers the big things.

Small advice

Don’t over-plan every hour. Pick 1–2 main things per day and leave the rest open. Sometimes the best moments are unplanned.
