Ubud reset: cafés, rice terraces, and one perfect sunrise

A calm itinerary for nature lovers who still want great food and easy day trips.

Asia Wellness 10/11/2025 6 min read

Day 1: Sunrise at Tegalalang, rice terraces and culture

Wake early for the sunrise hike through Tegalalang Rice Terraces. The morning light here is unbeatable, and you'll have the paths mostly to yourself. Grab breakfast at a warung afterward, then explore the nearby artisan villages and craft workshops.

  • Early morning: Tegalalang Rice Terraces sunrise walk.
  • Breakfast: Local warung with fresh juice and nasi goreng.
  • Afternoon: Tegallalang Art Village, wood carving studios, local galleries.

Day 2: Temples, yoga, and jungle bathing

Visit Goa Gajah (Elephant Cave Temple) in the morning, then head to a riverside yoga class. Afternoon is for Tegenungan Waterfall or a jungle swing. Ubud is best when you balance activity with complete relaxation.

Budget tips that keep the journey authentic

Stay in mid-range guesthouses in central Ubud for easy access. Eat at warungs instead of tourist cafés—you'll find incredible meals for a fraction of the price. Hire a scooter or local driver for day trips. Ubud's magic is in its simplicity.
