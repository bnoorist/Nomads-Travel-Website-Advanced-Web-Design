First time in NYC: a 3 day plan that balances classics and calm

Iconic walks, quick museum picks, and the neighborhoods you will want to repeat.

North America Guide 09/05/2025 6 min read

Day 1: Downtown classics and Brooklyn vibes

Start at Battery Park for lower Manhattan views, then walk through SoHo's cobblestone streets and historic neighborhoods. Cross the Brooklyn Bridge on foot during golden hour. End the day in Brooklyn Heights with dinner overlooking the skyline.

  • Morning: Battery Park, South Street Seaport, quick coffee stop.
  • Midday: SoHo galleries, Washington Square Park, local shops.
  • Evening: Walk Brooklyn Bridge, explore Dumbo and DUMBO waterfront.

Day 2 & 3: Museums, neighborhoods, and food scenes

Dedicate one day to a museum (MoMA, Natural History) and the other to neighborhood exploration. Hit Chelsea Market for breakfast, explore Greenwich Village, and sample food from different cultures in Manhattan's diverse neighborhoods.

Budget tips that maximize your time

Get a 7-day MetroCard for subway access. Eat from street vendors and small delis instead of tourist traps. Many museums have suggested-donation hours. Walk as much as possible—the neighborhoods reveal themselves best on foot.
